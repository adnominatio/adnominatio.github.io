An example post you can delete

15 January 2026

This is a template. Everything you need to write a post is on this page: a heading, a date, and paragraphs of text. To publish your own, copy this file, rename it (the date-first naming keeps them sorted), change the title and body, and add a line linking to it on the Writing page.

Because paths matter one folder deep, the stylesheet and nav links here start with ../ — that's the only difference from the top-level pages.

You can use headings

And ordinary formatting: bold, italic, and links. That's plenty for an occasional blog.
