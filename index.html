Casey Glick

R&D Fluidic Systems Engineer · Ph.D. Physics, UC Berkeley

I build and analyze fluidic instrumentation and control systems — translating physical theory into working hardware.

I'm a multidisciplinary R&D engineer with over a decade of experience in microfluidics, device fabrication, quality testing, and data analysis. My work spans fluidic instrumentation and controls, model-based performance testing, engineering feasibility studies, and design of experiments. I like problems that sit at the boundary between theory and a bench — where an order-of-magnitude estimate and a carefully built test rig have to agree.

Most recently my work has centered on cooling and control dynamics and fluidic delivery systems. Earlier, I designed catheter components and analysis tools at Laminar (Johnson & Johnson), optimized company-wide fluidic techniques at Illumina, developed fluidic control for haptic systems at Facebook Reality Labs, and completed a physics Ph.D. at UC Berkeley on microfluidic circuitry via additive manufacturing.
